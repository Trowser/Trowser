# Code of Conduct

Trowser is a small project. The bar for taking part is ordinary respect.

## Expected behavior

- Assume other people are here to test software well, and to improve this tool.
- Critique the work. Be specific. A repro, a log line, or a suggested wording is more useful than a verdict about the person who wrote it.
- Security findings need care. Discuss a flaw in Trowser. Do not post live credentials, customer data, or a working attack against someone else's system.
- Disagreement about product direction is welcome. [VISION.md](VISION.md) says what is settled and what is still open. Keep that argument about the product.

## Behavior that is not welcome

- Harassment, insults, and personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Spamming issues with generated reports that nobody has reproduced.

## Where this applies

Issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space that is held out as a Trowser project channel.

## Contact

If something in those spaces goes wrong and you do not want to post it publicly, email rikard.edgren@thetesteye.com. Reports will be read by the maintainer. The response can be a warning, a removed comment, or a block, depending on what happened.
