Trowser

Exploratory Tester's Companion

Understand what is important, then test it.

Trowser is a normal web browser, with F12 for DevTools, and also a lot of built-in quicktests and C# scripting console. You, and AI, strongest together, have full control and can collaborate to find new information.

1

Understand what matters

You need to learn your products qualities and risks. By exploring the software in various ways you will learn more, understand relations and find important quality-related information. You can turn on recording in the Journal, so you can concentrate fully on the software.

2

Test it, rich and diverse

We are in the sampling business. Requirements cover only a part of the full product, and important problems can be anywhere. Use Diverse half-measures, testing pretty good in different ways finds more than testing perfect in one or two ways. Explore on your own, get help from AI, run a few quicktests, then follow up on whatever looks odd.

In search of the potato

Try Ctrl+B to test blind with a screen curtain. Right-click an element for context-aware tests such as Click frenzy.

3

Communicate what you found

Testing is never better than the communication of the results. Write down qualities, problems, and risks while they are fresh, and share the story of your testing, not only the bug list. Train and use your judgment.
